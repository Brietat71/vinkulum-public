# Help sustain Vinkulum

Vinkulum is building an open engineering workbench: geometry, mechanics and
inspectable results today; a broader home for scientific solvers over time.
Sustaining that work requires protected maintainer time as well as contributors.

We are interested in discussing support for specific, publicly reviewable
milestones. No donation checkout or sponsorship programme has been activated
through this document.

The immediate priority is small contributions to the individual maintainer,
with a proposed first goal of €100 in one-time support. Creating an association
is not a prerequisite for preparing that appeal. The
[launch kit](SOUTENIR_MAINTENANT.md) includes French/English copy and payment
setup instructions. A verified payment link will be added after activation.

## What support would make possible

| Area | Deliverable a supporter can inspect |
|---|---|
| Maintainer continuity | Time for implementation, reviews, releases and dependency maintenance |
| Reliable desktop releases | Tested Linux/macOS packages and repeatable installation recipes |
| Solver integration | One connector with a documented model contract and reference comparisons |
| Scientific validation | Open benchmark models, independently reproduced results and stated tolerances |
| Research contributions | A scoped student/research project with agreed supervision and deliverables |
| Performance | Published profiles, a targeted implementation and reproducible before/after measurements |

A useful first conversation names a use case, a deliverable and an acceptance
test. A lab may bring reference data and validation expertise; an engineering
team may bring an integration use case; a sponsor may fund maintenance time.
The objective is reusable public work that strengthens the project.

To start a non-confidential discussion, open a
[project issue](https://github.com/Brietat71/vinkulum-public/issues/new).
Commercial details and any contractual commitments should be handled separately.

## Funding preparation

The maintainer is based in France. A French association is the initial vehicle
proposed for discussion; it has not been created. The
[France funding note](FINANCEMENT_FRANCE.md) compares the options, addresses
paid maintenance and outlines the legal/tax points to resolve. Build a costed
milestone plan with separate lines for maintainer remuneration, external
contributions, hardware/testing, hosting and administration.

Candidate routes to investigate are recurring sponsorship, funded open-source
milestones, research partnerships and grants, and paid integration/support work.
Eligibility, deadlines, tax treatment and payment mechanisms are not established
here and must be checked for the actual applicant. No funding or income is
promised. An initial budget should come from real effort and costs, not GitHub
star counts.

The current public code's Apache-2.0 licence remains in place. External solvers
and libraries retain their own licences. Funding proposals should identify
what will be public, the acceptance criteria, ownership and ongoing maintenance
responsibility before work starts.
