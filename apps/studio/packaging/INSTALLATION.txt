Vinkulum Studio 0.5.0 — Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later

Drag Vinkulum Studio into Applications, then launch it. Python, the native
kernel, Qt and VTK are bundled; no local compilation is needed. This is
experimental software; the complete mechanics kernel is not certified.
Consult the accompanying macOS qualification report for the actual build.
Linux qualification does not establish macOS compatibility.

The build uses ad-hoc signing, without a Developer ID certificate or Apple
notarisation. macOS may refuse automatic opening and require explicit approval
in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Do not disable global security settings.
